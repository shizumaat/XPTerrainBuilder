# Reading v2 patches from the app (2026-09-04, lane v2app)

Two auto-patch engines ship side by side (RULINGS 2026-09-03d, 2026-09-04g):
**v1** (`src/auto_patch`, the shipping default) and **v2** (`src/auto_patch_v2`,
the ground-up law-compliant rewrite — 0/0 on CYXY/SPLP/SPJC/OTHH/LEMD
through the harness). The app selects the engine through ONE setting.

## Switch the engine

* App → Build settings → **Elevation** → **Auto-patch engine (v1 / v2
  preview)** — a per-tile setting (global default + per-tile override, like
  every other tile setting). In the config files it is the flat key
  `auto_patch_engine = v2` (`Ortho4XP.cfg` for all tiles, or
  `Tiles/zOrtho4XP_+25+051/Ortho4XP_+25+051.cfg` for one).
* Rebuild the tile. Every auto-patch carries the engine that wrote it in
  its freshness stamp (`o4_ap_engine`), so flipping the key REBUILDS the
  tile's auto-patches with the other engine — no manual deletion, no
  `O4_AUTO_PATCH_REBUILD`, and never a reused patch from the wrong engine.
* The build log says which engine ran, once per tile
  (`Auto-patch: engine v2 …`) and once per airport:
  `[provenance] OTHH patch: engine=v2 sha=… law=4e82b5bada40 ruleset=icao
  solve=optimal dem=production[N25E051] insets=…`. `law=` is the digest of
  the six TOML law tables the patch was solved under.
* A v2 solve the law cannot satisfy, or a v2 refusal (cold DEM frame,
  missing law tables), is a **named per-airport FAILURE** — the tile build
  stops, the app shows the airport and stage (`solve` / `build`), and the
  IIS text is in `Patches/<block>/<tile>/auto_patch_verify_debug.log`.
  Nothing is silently skipped and no stale patch is left for the mesh.
  (The "no apt.dat for this CIFP airport" skip is unchanged — that is not
  a build the tile owes.)

## What to rebuild and what to look for

* **+25+051 (OTHH)** — the owner's sites: the Dewatering_01/02 basins
  (floors −9.06 / −10.74 with the 3.96 retaining crest, RULINGS 2026-09-04f),
  the tunnel mouths (8–9 of 10 canonical under v1; v2's M4 readers), the
  apron ↔ taxiway welds and the runway crowns. Also OTBD and OTBH on the
  same tile (both v2-optimal on 2026-09-04: 13.7 s and 7.0 s).
* **+60-136 (CYXY)** — the cheap control: 4–5 s per build, seam tear
  class was v2's last residual (fixed 04g); compare the runway 14L/32R
  longitudinal profile and the apron edges against v1.
* **−13−077 / −13−078 (SPJC, SPLP)** — SPLP straddles the tile seam:
  each tile's build places its OWN piece (`write_tile_pieces`), so build
  BOTH tiles and read the seam band at the −77/−78 line for a tear.
* Look for: steps at pavement edges (v2 has none by construction — the
  planar map shares vertices), the 3–5 % end-lip on runway strips, groundside
  ring vertices carrying the lip with roads exempt (04g OPEN item), pads
  yielding to their apron over real relief (03h), and mesh triangle density
  (v2 should show FEWER constrained edges than v1 on the same tile).

## A/B against v1

1. Build the tile with `auto_patch_engine = v1`; copy the tile's DSF aside
   (or install it as a differently-named pack).
2. Set `auto_patch_engine = v2`; rebuild (only the auto-patches and the
   mesh rebuild; imagery is reused). Both patches' provenance lines are in
   the log; the two `Patches/<block>/<tile>/<ICAO>_auto.patch.osm` files can
   be censused with `tools/harness/census.py` — v1 OTHH 1,620 airside vs v2 0.
3. Fly the same approaches; the harness twin of this A/B is
   `build_airport.py OTHH --tile 25 51 --engine v2` (geometry half; the
   imagery half stands down without a provider).
