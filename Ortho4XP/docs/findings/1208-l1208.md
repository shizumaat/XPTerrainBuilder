## The rule (one site)

The stamp (core, claimed-object clusters, R21 isthmus) bakes Z0 through
`_bake_one_inset`; every bake of one airport is followed by ONE pass,
`auto_patch/flat_site_relief.restore_ground_above_flat_level`, against a
snapshot of the honest surface taken before that airport's bakes:

* a post the stamp LOWERED whose honest surface stands more than
  `KEEP_ABOVE_M` = 0.5 m above Z0 gets its honest surface back: in full
  from `REAL_FROM_M` = 1.0 m, linearly between 0.5 and 1.0 m (so the
  surface is continuous and no post ends more than 0.5 m below the
  honest surface);
* posts the stamp raised (honest surface below Z0) keep Z0 — the fill
  (20af, R8-1);
* the airport's own PAVEMENT (apt.dat pavements ∪ runways, margin 0 —
  the decision record's new `pavement` / `pavement_ll`) stays at Z0
  whatever the DEM says: that is the pavement-grading cut the ruling
  keeps.  The boundary's grass, the margin, cluster hulls and the
  isthmus are no longer exempt (the #1137 `gate` exemption is retired).
