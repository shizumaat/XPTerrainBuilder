## The rule (one site) — two readings measured

The stamp (core, claimed-object clusters, R21 isthmus) bakes Z0 through
`_bake_one_inset`; every bake of one airport is followed by ONE pass,
`auto_patch/flat_site_relief.restore_ground_above_flat_level`, against a
snapshot of the honest surface taken before that airport's bakes (it
replaces the #1137 `restore_off_site_relief`, which used a 5 m / 10 m
band):

* a post the stamp LOWERED whose honest surface stands more than
  `KEEP_ABOVE_M` = 0.5 m above Z0 gets its honest surface back: in full
  from `REAL_FROM_M` = 1.0 m, linearly between 0.5 and 1.0 m (so the
  surface is continuous and no post ends more than 0.5 m below the
  honest surface — a mechanism choice; a hard step at 0.5 m is the
  alternative);
* posts the stamp raised keep Z0 — the fill (20af, R8-1), unchanged
  byte for byte (24.5 km2 of raised posts on +22+113 identical);
* an exempt SITE keeps Z0 whatever the DEM says. Which site is the
  owner's call — "except where pavement grading itself needs a cut":
  * **arm A (literal, ruled by 2026-10-27d):** apt.dat pavements ∪ runways only (commit
    `ec614da9c`, which also re-scopes `check_plateau` to the pavement);
  * **arm B (not landed; Q184 ruled it out):** the airport's own surfaces, pavement ∪
    runways ∪ boundary (the existing `gate_ll`); the plateau only fills
    in the margin, the cluster hulls and the isthmus.
