## The rule (one reader for all three: the HANGING pack object)

A pack object whose solids hang ≥ `shell_floor_min_m` below its zero with no
plate of `plate_min_area_m2` at its walls' bottom (no floor) is a HANGING object;
its depth is its walls' bottom (Q167; 20am "the bottom of the object is at the
road"; 20ar the object wins over the lidar road).

1. **A hanging object with a flush HARD DECK (|y| ≤ `cover_flush_m`) is a COVER**
   (Q165: the pack's deck carries the airside).  It is read as a §33 (6) B shell
   with ITSELF as the cover; a co-placed FLOOR object overlapping it (a deep
   plate of a plate's worth) is read WITH it as one tunnel (Q166), the floor
   then the authored floor plate, else the wall bottoms (Q167).  The trench is
   cut under the deck (19p, 19e (3)), the deck seated flush with the solved
   surface it carries (26d `cut_deck_seat`).
2. **A hanging object with NO hard deck over a mapped bore end is a PORTAL**
   (Q168; 20at "faces just make tunnel mouths"; 20ao edge-only objects end the
   road at their back): the #1343 pin, with
   - the depth = its walls' bottom below its top, read from the ground the top
     stands flush with — the highest DEM under the object's plan (20am "the top
     is level with the taxiway");
   - the cut taking in the object (20an / 20as): the mouth moves into the bore
     to the object's back (its plan's furthest extent along the bore);
   - no `mouth_depth_m` cap (20ar: the object sets the depth); the 26k strip
     exemption applies.
3. The roofed portal of #1343 (KBUF) is unchanged.
