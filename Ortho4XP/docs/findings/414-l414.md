end segment at `axis[0]` / `axis[-1]` — the end of the pair's MIDLINE —
with the half-widths of the terminal STATION (`sts[0]` / `sts[-1]`).

SHORTER wall ends, so for arms of unequal length the segment stood half the

of an end use that one segment: rule 4's family cover (`_end_cover`, the
`closed` list, the class) and §12h's mouth (`wall_mouth.admit`: W1s/W3
bands, FIELD, the mouth ground).

Second defect of the same function, found reading it: at end 0 the segment's
normal was built from `axis[0] - axis[1]` (pointing OUT of the corridor), so
`half_l` was laid on the RIGHT and `half_r` on the left — the segment was
mirrored across the axis by `half_l - half_r`. At end 1 it was right. The
station's own crossing (`stations_along.at`) is the one segment that spans
the two inner faces at that station.

## Rule (general; no airport named)

Each end `k` of a wall-corridor candidate is tested on ONE segment: the
crossing of its TERMINAL STATION (`sts[0]` at end 0, `sts[-1]` at end 1) —
the axis point at the station's `s`, its normal taken exactly as
`stations_along` takes it (the axis direction over ±1 m about `s`), from
`half_l` on the left to `half_r` on the right. Rule 4's cover, W1s, W3,
FIELD and the mouth ground all read that segment. The replay's floor-road
probe (measure only) reads the mouth at the same station point.

One helper, `tunnel_walls.station_crossing(axis_ln, st)`, is that segment;
`stations_along.at` and `read_family` both call it so the station and the
end test cannot drift apart.
