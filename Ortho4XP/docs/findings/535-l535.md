## Rule

(d) stands only where one of the five witnesses shows the tunnel
(`planar/structure_fence.fence_witness`), else the bore is refused by name:

1. rise ≥ `[tunnel] terrain_rise_m` over the mouth chord;
2. road level: a road joined at a mouth (within `[tunnel]
   terrain_crossing_min_m`, (c)'s approach distance) carries non-zero
   `layer`, `bridge`, `tunnel` or `incline`; or the bore carries `incline`;
3. pack portal: a pack part based deeper than `[basin] contact_band_m`
   under its zero within `[tunnel.object] bore_end_tolerance_m` of a
   mouth, or a pack tunnel corridor's footprint within that distance;
4. over: a mapped way (road, railway, aeroway, building; not a tunnel)
   across the bore's interior at any layer, or a pack building part at
   grade holding `terrain_cover_min_m` of its line (10i (1)'s cover);
5. lidar: a credible inset reads a cut along it (`channel_geometry._lidar_cut`).

The portal numbers are existing law, replacing the wrapper's 30 m / -0.5 m
(scratch, not law): 5 m is the distance at which RULINGS 2026-09-08o reads a
bore end as a pack tunnel object's mouth; 1.0 m is the ground-contact band
below which pack geometry is below grade. No new law key.
