## 1. Mechanism

### W1 Wings (airport/portal_objects.py, one site)
New private reader beside _front_wall_top (:299):

    _wing_plan(o, g, roof, local, top_y, end_xy, into, law) -> plan | None

A WING is a solid of the portal's OWN object that
1. stands AT the stated top: a non-animated solid triangle of g.solid whose highest vertex
   is >= top_y - [tunnel.object] cover_flush_m. ALL components are read: a thin plate at the
   top is a cap, not a ground decal, so ResourceCache.genuine is not applied here;
2. taken in plan: plan-bearing triangles as polygons (obj8_clip._bulk_polys), and every
   candidate triangle's outline as a line grown by a module constant _WALL_HALF_M = 0.1
   (a wall stands edge-on); frame_entry.union, then frame_entry.enter;
3. less the WHOLE roof grown by 2 * _WALL_HALF_M;
4. a polygon part is kept only if it stands within plate_bin_m of `local` (the roof over
   THIS end: continuous with it) AND runs at least entrance_skirt_m OUT of the bore beyond
   that roof's front (front = the least station of local's rings along `into` from end_xy;
   run = front - the part's least station);
5. None where no part is kept, where `into` is None, or where local is empty.

Call it in the tall branch right after `front` (:568-569), only where front is not None.
At :573:
    face_plan = roof if wing is None else roof.union(wing)      (still only where j in cover)
Nothing else on the Portal changes: face_top_z, top_plan, footprint, opening_m and opening_xy
stay as R3 left them. Update the face_plan field comment (:106-112).

### W2 Corridor (planar/entrance_skirt.py, one site; planar/structures.py, one call)
entrance_skirt(..., outward: "XY | None" = None). Inside the existing
`if not is_entrance_face(pin):` block (:170-176), when outward is given and the ramp is not
empty, `held` gains the ramp's plan SWEPT along outward by the diagonal of reach.bounds,
grown by stand_off (mitre). The sweep is exact (the plan, its translate, and each ring edge's
parallelogram: private _swept(geom, d)), never a convex hull. It keeps the level piece and
the bank off alike (it joins `blocked`). planar/structures.py:1337-1338 passes
outward=(-inward[0], -inward[1]). An entrance face never enters that block.

Do NOT keep the corridor off the level piece only: measured, that drops 3 TL ramp nodes,
re-cuts big_roads:-1989 and changes ENTC.
