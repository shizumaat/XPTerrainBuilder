## 1. Mechanism (Part 1)

### R1 The frame is the object (airport/portal_objects.py)
Private `_frame(o, objects, cache, foot, law) -> (vertices, tris)`: the portal and every placement
its seat leads -- `hanging_objects._co_placed(o, q, tol)` and `walled_pit._in_pit(q, foot, cache,
reach)`, with the SAME tol and reach pipeline/rebake_inputs hands
`terrain_shaped.portal_frame_records` (read them there; one source, no second grouping) -- their
still solids (`g.solid` less `g.animated`, every component, no `genuine` filter: R4 W1's reason)
concatenated in the portal's authored frame (one anchor and heading). Called once per tall pin
with a floor (`j in cover`).

### R2 The cover and the soffit (same file)
Private `_covers(frame, end_xy, into, o, law)`: the frame's near-horizontal faces
(`plate_normal_y_min`) whose top stands at least `bridge.clearance_minimum_m` over the zero,
binned by `plate_bin_m`. A bin is a COVER BIN where its plan union (one-sided area, never the
triangle sum) is at least `plate_min_area_m2`, is a PLATE and not a wall top
(`plan.buffer(-wall_face_max_thickness_m / 2)` is not empty: the #2099 W1 witness), and comes
within `bore_end_tolerance_m` of the bore end once placed.
- THE COVER = the highest cover bin: `(top y, plan)`, the plan = every near-horizontal face of
  that bin (thin fringes too), placed.
- THE SOFFIT = the lowest cover bin's plan, placed.
- No cover bin: None; the pin reads exactly as R4.

### R3 What the Portal states (same file, :632-645)
New fields `cover_plan` (frame metres) and `cover_top_z` (= floor + cover top y); None for every
other pin. `face_plan` = cover_plan ∪ wing (R4's roof ∪ wing with the cover for the roof).
`_front_wall_top`, `_wing_plan`, `face_top_z`, `top_plan`, `footprint`, `floor_z`, `depth_m`:
computed as R4 from the object's own roof, unchanged. New field `parapet_plan`: the plan (placed)
of the frame's solids whose highest vertex stands more than `plate_bin_m` over the cover top;
None where none.

### R4 The opening where the road pins the end up the open road (:620-623, the `off > tol` arm only)
`_roof_opening(soffit, xy, into, tol)` where a soffit exists, else today's circle reading. The
`off <= tol` arm is untouched.

### M1 The cap never stands beyond the cover's back (planar/structure_mouth.py; structures.py:558-568)
For a pin with cover_plan (never an entrance, footed, hanging, edge or deck pin): after
`portal_mouth` as today, read the cover's BACK: on each line along `inward` across the ramp
(`entrance_skirt._deepest_front`'s sampling over the opening's halves) the greatest station of
cover_plan; the back = the least of those. If the mouth's station + the cap stand-off (`rim_off +
grid`, the stand-off `structure_geometry.geometry` reaches on a snapped ring) is greater than the
back, cut the axis back out of the bore to back - stand-off, with `roof_back_mouth`'s own
convention (`substring`, negative `moved`), so `covered_stretch`, `own_road_blade` and
`road_end_after_slide` read it as they read a footed portal's. If the built cap still stands
beyond the back, one more grid step, at most three (as the builder). Otherwise the mouth stays.
One function; extend `roof_back_mouth` with a set-back argument rather than write a second.

### S1 No skirt in front of a parapet (planar/entrance_skirt.entrance_skirt, the roofed block :194-205)
`held` gains `_swept(pin.parapet_plan, outward * far)` (NOT grown by stand_off): level piece and
bank alike. The ground in front of a wall that rises over the cover is never skirt.

### S2 Two levels (same function; planar/structures.py:1432-1442 and :1532-1545)
The level piece is built as R4 (`_skirt_cells` on face_plan). Where the pin has wings (face_plan
is more than cover_plan), split it on the line across the bore through the cover's front
(`_wing_plan`'s own `front`):
- the part into the bore from the line is the COVER'S piece: ref `entrance_skirt:<tid>` (as
  today), level cover_top_z;
- the part out of it is the WINGS' piece: new ref `entrance_wing:<tid>` (third ref from
  `skirt_refs`), level face_top_z (R4);
- a strip `min_distinct_spacing_m` wide astride the line belongs to neither.
No wings: one piece, the cover's, at cover_top_z. `entrance_skirt` returns the wings' piece and
level as extra members; `Tunnel.entrance_skirt = (cover_top_z, core ref, bank ref)`; new field
`Tunnel.entrance_wing = (face_top_z, ref)` (default `()`); `skirt_of[tid]` carries every part.

### S3 Rows (constraints/entrance_skirt.skirt_pins)
The wings' piece's ring vertices are pinned at its level by the same rule as the level piece
(senior, never an airside vertex, the `held` hand-off unchanged).
