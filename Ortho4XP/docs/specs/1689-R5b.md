## M1 The cap never stands beyond the cover's back (replaces R5 M1; structures.py:570-578,
## structure_mouth._cap_under_cover :885, entrance_skirt._cover_back :114)
1 Applies to a pin with cover_plan that is not an entrance, footed, hanging, edge or deck pin, and
  only where portal_mouth left the mouth on the mapped end (moved == 0.0).
2 The back, from the middle: `_line_stations` (own inset and step) over the middle half of the
  ramp: halves ((3·left − right)/4, (3·right − left)/4); back = least over those lines of the
  cover's greatest station.
3 No action (0.00) where any sampled middle line meets no cover, or on any of them the cover's
  least station is above 0 or its greatest below 0. `_line_stations` yields a miss for a line
  that meets nothing; `_deepest_front` skips misses (its result unchanged).
4 If back >= rim_off + grid (2.1) the mouth stays. Otherwise s = back − (rim_off + grid); while
  the cap's centre, snapped as the builder snaps it (snap_out(c + inward·rim_off, c, grid), c the
  moved mouth), stands beyond the back, s −= grid, at most three times. Cut the axis with
  roof_back_mouth's convention (substring, negative moved).
5 Bound: no M1 move exceeds rim_off + 4·grid = 3.6 m.
6 Skew: the cap is square to the road; the M1 note carries the back and its range across the
  whole ramp. (Shearing the cap onto the back is a follow-up.)
