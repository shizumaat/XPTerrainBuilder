## 2. Rule A — a retaining wall is seated on its upper ground (item 1)
New member `airport/retaining_seat.py` (`retaining_seats`): every wall-class component that RETAINS,
whose retained face reaches a stated height of its own (`WallComponent.retained_top_y`, its zero for a
depth or buried witness), retains more than `retaining_wall_clear_step_m`, and is seated by no other
rule (tunnel crest, basin, authored seat, walled plate, mouth wing, storey shell, model) gets ONE plate
entry: y = `retained_top_y`, stations `min_distinct_spacing_m` outside its UPPER face (`wall_sides`'
own `_wall_faces` / `_upper_index`) every `abutment_sample_step_m`, kind `RETAINING_PLATE`
(`pipeline/rebake_inputs._retaining_plates`, after the mouth wings). `anchor_rule.datum_of` reads the
kind as a datum (as the edge / walled / split-level plates): the anchor at the median station, the zero
on its ground. Its footing goes where that puts it (13u (1)).
A2 (found by the closing build): the §16g unit pass and the 22o group zero re-seated the body by its
feet ("one connected group fu:132:1483 at 38.73"), over the datum. `airport/open_frame_seat.LEAD_PLATES`
= {walled, retaining}: a retaining plate leads its unit (`walled_unit_zero`) and its group
(`walled_lead`, which now reads the plate's datum on the surface when the unit pass re-anchored the
body), exactly as a runway-end walled plate (#1201). MEASURED (object-stage replay

## 4. Rule C — a structure floor is no zone lip reference (item 3)
