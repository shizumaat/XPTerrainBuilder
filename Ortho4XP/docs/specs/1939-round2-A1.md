## A1 — no vertex of a class-K ramp is ungoverned (replaces R2's "shared with ground: no row")

A K mouth digs nothing (RULINGS 2026-11-13h N1), so its ramp is the survey at every vertex (2026-10-30a (2),
10-30f (1)). For each K ramp vertex off the mouth edge:
- (a) Bare: senior pin at the DEM at the vertex (unchanged).
- (b) Shared, and the ground carries a value there: no row. That is: any sharing face is airside or pad
  (runway, taxi, apron, pad or platform level — stage 1 owns it; a DEM pin there is the LEMD infeasibility the
  ground rule was written for), or the vertex has a published target (`pm.road_ramp_z`, `pm.preferred_z`,
  `taxi_trend_z`).
- (c) Shared only with groundside road or lot faces that publish no target at the vertex: senior pin at the
  DEM at the vertex, Source head `tunnel.survey_floor`.
§37 (6) untouched (a route vertex with a ramp target is case (b)); 2026-11-03b untouched (no depth: the top is
natural ground). The predicate lives in ONE place, `survey_floor.py`; `shared_with_ground` is not edited. No
airport, way or ref named.
