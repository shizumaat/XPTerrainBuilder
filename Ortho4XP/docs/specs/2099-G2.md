## 4. STOP -- what the next round needs (owner / reviewer)

The reclassification is right by the owner's words and moves only pol88/pol89's roles, but
three consumers break and the brief's airside bar cannot hold:

Q1 AIRSIDE COLLATERAL. Accept that taxiway pav133 (and stage-1 neighbours to 400 m, worst
0.51 m) re-solve when pol89 leaves airside, or keep pol89 in stage 1 as a level-only
participant (a new class: groundside role, airside solve) -- a law question.
Q2 THE CAR PARK ON THE DECK. ~15,800 m2 of pol88/pol89 lie inside the M de-ice deck's
(obj10884) ring. A lot cannot own ground under an airside hard deck today. Options: (a) the
part of a lot inside a hard deck's ring stays the deck's (split the lot at the ring, deck
side airside); (b) a lot may stand on a hard deck (its ground held flush to the deck, as the
deck ground is). Either is a planar rule at one derivation site; (a) re-adds airside.
Q3 THE OWNER'S ROAD THROUGH THE LOT. Under 10-14a (2) the A -> B road is the lot and graded
at 5 %; "the road carries the remainder of the climb" needs its course minted as a road
(10 % cap) inside the lot -- an amendment to 10-14a (2) for a mapped road from a ramp road
into a lot at a different level.
Q4 THE TUNNEL RAMP EXTENT. Why the M ramp's tunnel_ramp now runs to s = 122 at 1 % (under
the foot) is not attributed here (`planar/structures` ramp-top / wall-framed climb reads the
changed ground); it must be read before any Part G implementation.
