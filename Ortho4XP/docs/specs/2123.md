## 12. Round #2181 / #2183 (lane lkphx2181, 2026-11-19; owner sim reads 14:03Z KPHX, 14:31-14:33Z KIAH, 14:04Z KORD / KRDU)

General rules, no thresholds of their own (every bound is an existing law key):
1. **The cover ends where OSM maps the road open** (`planar/deck_gen_cover.covered_state`): an underpass road grows (deviation 2) only through pieces mapped in its own state (`tunnel` / `covered` / `layer` < 0 vs open); an OPEN piece joined to a covered one is open sky between two bridges, no deck over it. A way read from two tiles' extracts counts once (`distinct_ways`). KPHX Sky Harbor Blvd: 126 m "tunnel" -> 65 m under each bridge.
2. **The deck carries the taxiway over the pack's hole** (`bridge_ribbons`): a mapped aeroway bridge over no taxi/runway pavement, with pavement beyond both ends within `level_probe_m`, is pavement for the deck at the joined pavement's half width (capped at the authority's widest `taxi.width_m`); the ring spans the hole pavement to pavement. `structure_underpass.underpass_bores`: along a bridge where no deck cell's stations reach and no airside cell stands, the clip is the taxiway's default width, never a road's lanes fallback (KIAH `-414`: 0 roads bored).
3. **One mapped bridge, one deck** (`deck_gen_pairs.one_bridge`): ring pieces under one OSM aeroway bridge are joined (their hull).
4. **Side by side, one cut** (`one_cut`): two generated decks over one mapped road whose rings stand within their two banks' runs (floor depth / `deck_bank_slope`, 18 m at 6 m) are one cut: the ground between them over the roads' cut joins both decks' `approach` (airside pavement leaves, pack pages clip), `deck_partner` pairs them, the floor runs level. Farther apart each is its own cut (Q271 b).
5. **A road bridge beside a taxiway deck rides it or gets its own** (`riders`; owner 14:33Z): a generated road bridge over a road the deck covers, its carriageway within `2 x edge_margin_m` of the deck's carried pavement (the two rings would meet), widens the deck and is not built (`DeckBridge.riders`); farther off it is its own deck. KPHX `-1818` / `-1811`: 0.0 m (ride); KIAH `-1318`: 45.2 m (own deck).
6. **A pack deck high over the crossing refuses nothing** (`deck_pack_cover.overhead_decks`): a pack hard deck whose rendered top stands `[bridge] clearance_minimum_m` or more over the piece's ground passes OVER the crossing (KPHX `SkyTrain.obj` over taxiway bridge `-96`).
7. **Road over our cut** (`road_bridge_gen._into_deck_cut`, B1 (b) widened): a mapped road bridge no pack page covers is generated where a lower road it crosses runs, within the cut's approach run (`_approach_run_m`) along its mapped chain, under a mapped aeroway bridge. KPHX `-301` / `-302`, KIAH `-1318`, `-1337`, `-1317`.
8. **Form** (owner 14:31Z / 14:32Z, every airport): a generated road bridge is a NARROW FLAT DECK (`pipeline/road_deck`: ring = axis +- carriageway half + `edge_margin_m`, end lines at the mapped ends, top straight between the ends' embankment levels, family `road`), written by the taxiway deck's writer; no X-Plane road chain is written.
