authorising no write. `Inputs.dem_frame = "production" | "authored"`
(fixtures and probes stay authored).

## 4. RAOA and within_shape adjudication (M2 open questions 1–2)

(`scratchpad raoa_hops.py`, the same triple loop as `_check_raoa_rate`)
and classifying every over-cap triple by its hops: **CYXY 28/28 and SPLP

Annex 14 §3.8.4 reads profiles along the approach. The reader fix
(`tools/check_grade.py`, commit `91426d6c`, SEPARATE — take or leave):
compute the lateral station and skip a triple with a cross-width hop;

292 pass. With it, CYXY reads **33 → 2** and SPLP **6 → 0** on the oracle;
v2's `verify/raoa` applies the same rule. Verdict: v1 instrument, not the
strip.

**within_shape apron (3 chords, 1.02–1.09 % over 30–38 m).** RULINGS
