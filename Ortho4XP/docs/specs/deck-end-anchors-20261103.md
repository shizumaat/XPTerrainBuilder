## §2.0 Scope (owner 16:08:44Z)
A DeckAnchor is minted ONLY for a pack object with a hard deck (ATTR_hard_deck flag, or the #1712 signature deck) whose end lines the airport's own pavement runs across (27c). No OSM bridge=yes way, no mapped tunnel, no lidar cut, no edge-wall-only bridge (27l), no terrain deck (Deck.datum != "deck_top") mints one. Without such an object every airside row is as today: the knife keeps its whole-airside exemption (structures.py:361-366, structure_service.py:734-828), the ridge is not cut, no pin, and the bore's cut stops at the airside edge (§34 (12)(3) airside_cut_roles, structures.py:369). ## Bridge ends bank by default (owner 2026-11-03T16:56:16Z)
Owner, verbatim: "Unless there's evidence such as a wall object or road edge requiring a vertical cut under the bridge, assume a sloping embankment coming down from bridge ends to the trench bottom." A hard deck without walls already banks from the road edge up to just short of the deck (Q-A as ruled); a deck whose frame models walls is vertical at them (30f (3), the witness). Under a slab the floor now stops `depth / [design] bank_slope` (1:3) short of the deck's edge, so the ground comes down from the deck to the trench floor as an embankment. The sidecar records which (`deck_anchors[*].cut`: `bank` / `walls` / `slab`).
