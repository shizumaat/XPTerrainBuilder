## §1 THE RUNWAY FLEXES — A HARD DEVIATION BUDGET OF 20 % OF THE PULL (30as; supersedes "stage 0")

**Numbers first.** HECA base replay 19,450 unknowns / 123,305 rows (30aa); stage 0 cut unknowns to 16,850 and moved 844 runway vertices, worst 2.547 m (30aj). Under this §1 unknowns stay **19,450** (the runway remains free); rows grow by **2 per runway-family column of a PULLED runway** — a `Band` is two one-sided rows (`solve/rows.py:241-245`) — at most 2 × 2,610 = **5,220** at HECA (+4.2 %), **0** on any airport with no held block. `runway_flex_share = 0.20`; 30ae's HECA 1.20 m mover would have been capped at 0.20 × its route's lift.

**Mechanism — a BOUNDED RUNWAY DEVIATION BUDGET, not a weight ratio.** (1) **Pass 1a** = today's stage 1 (`solve/design.py:844-847`, `airside_stage_roles`) with every `frontage_hold_rows` row (`constraints/platform.py:492`) DROPPED. Its runway values `z¹ᵃ` are the runway's UNPULLED profile — the same runway the sw1002 references carry (hold OFF on main), shaped as today by its pins, chord at 300 (`emit.toml:339`, priced `design.py:386-393`), hard relatives (`emit.toml:792-796`) and the apron/taxi rows 30aj named. On an airport with no held block pass 1a IS stage 1 and nothing below runs: 0 rows, 0 movers, byte-identical. (2) **The lift.** For held block `b` with datum `D_b` (chosen at the derivation site, §2) and contact `c`: `Λ_c = |D_b − z¹ᵃ_c|`, the displacement the hold demands at `c`. `c`'s route to the runway = `routes.route_path` (`constraints/routes.py:926`) from `c` to its least-budget runway-family column on §2's pair graph; the runway it lands on is `R(c)`. (3) **The budget.** `β_R = runway_flex_share · max{Λ_c : R(c) = R}` (0 for a runway no route reaches); the pulling contact `c*_R` is the argmax, its block the pulling pad. (4) **The rows.** EVERY runway-family column `p` of `R` (the columns `runway_stage_split` walks on fbf0d57c, `precedence.toml:35-36` `role_family == "runway"`) gets ONE HARD `Band(p, z¹ᵃ_p − β_R, z¹ᵃ_p + β_R)` (`model/constraints.py:114`), head `rulesets.runway.flex_budget` in `[design] hard_rulings` (`emit.toml:792`), so the runway can TRANSLATE between its pins by at most `β_R` — every column, because 30ae's mover came through 110 hops of crown / K / transverse relatives, which stay HARD (30ak/30am: pins `runway_profile.py:239`/`:152`, vertical curve `:545`, longitudinal `:264`, the continuous transverse bound `law/tables.py:262-285`, the §50 yield envelope `design.py:852-870`) and would carry an unbanded column's translation to the rest. A pinned column's Band is moot (the `Pin` is a constant). (5) **Pass 1b** = stage 1 again (the `_stage1(cs_x)` re-solve closure already at `design.py:862-867` is the pattern) with the hold rows, the datum Bands and the runway Bands in `cs`; `levels` for stage 2 read from pass 1b. **The runway's flex is `z¹ᵇ − z¹ᵃ` on its columns**, reported per runway (§6 A1, sidecar `runway_flex`). No weight changes: chord 300, `taxi_trend` 30 (`emit.toml:410`), `apron_trend` 30 (`:445`), `law` 300 (`:367`) all stand — the pull already reaches the runway through them (30ae), and §5's HARD fronting caps are what push a demand the taxi cannot carry within 1.5 % onto the runway's 20 %.

**One derivation site, one key.** `[design] runway_flex_share = 0.20` (`emit.toml`, beside `chord` at `:339`; `law/design_schema.py` field) is read ONLY in `constraints/no_step.hold_interval` (§2), which returns the datum Bands, the runway Bands and the per-runway record together; `solve_design` (`design.py:785`) calls it between pass 1a and 1b. The share is a VALUE, never a switch: `0.0` reproduces stage 0's law with today's runway as the origin.

**Why a budget and not a 4:1 weight ratio.** A weight is a per-vertex stiffness: the displacement share it yields along a route depends on the route's hop count, on which one-sided caps are active and on the chord-vs-trend contest at each station (30ae: 300 vs 30, 110 hops) — the share would differ pad to pad and could not be pre-registered, and "a weight contest is not a law" (`emit.toml:802-803`, the ramp-ceiling and bore-datum precedents). The budget states the owner's number exactly, per route, and its residual is a hard-set row the report already names (`design.py:1093-1103`). Why not stage 0: 30aj — the reference runway is partly apron-shaped (2.55 m at HECA), so a runway solved alone moves it, and the owner ruled the runway a solved member (30as (2)). Why not one-way pad rows: unchanged from the prior §1 — the hold never touches a runway column; the lag is not a fixed value (§20b).

**The runway's own laws are senior to the budget.** Pins, K, longitudinal, transverse, §50 yield stay hard; the Band is intersected with them. A Band that cannot be met with the hold hard is what §2's interval prevents by construction; if the hard-set report still shows a violated runway row or Band, that is §7's STOP, never a wider budget.

## §2 THE FEASIBILITY INTERVAL

**Definition.** For block `b` with hold set `C_b` = its held welded contacts (`constraints/platform.platform_contacts`, ramp contacts excluded, branch `frontage_hold_rows` `constraints/platform.py:492`) ∪ its plateau vertices (§3):

`I_b = ∩_{c ∈ C_b} [ max_p (z_p − B(p,c)), min_p (z_p + B(p,c)) ]`

over every ANCHOR `p`: each runway-family column at its pass-1a value `z¹ᵃ_p` (§1) widened by its budget — the runway term is `[z¹ᵃ_p − β_R − B(p,c), z¹ᵃ_p + β_R + B(p,c)]` — plus every stage-1 `Pin` (EAT pins `eat.py:254`, seam pins `seams.py:62`) at width 0; `B(p,c)` = the least-budget path in the **PAIR GRAPH** `G`: one edge per stage-1 `Diff` cap row between airside columns, budget `cap · d` — taxi longitudinal / transverse (`taxi.py:221`, `:263`; `transverse.py:141`), taxi_box pairs (`taxi.py:412`), apron ring-edge / frontage-chord / body chords at 1.5 % (`apron.py:214-220`), no-step pairs (`no_step.py:268`), the 5 % / 8 % ceilings (`pavement_cap.py:74`). Not the route graph: it reaches an apron contact only by a station hop and not at all on `band_roles` faces (fact 4). The pair graph is the rows themselves, so a datum inside `I_b` is EXACTLY one for which a Lipschitz extension of every pair cap exists between the fixed set and the hold set (McShane–Whitney on a metric graph). **Precondition:** every fixed pair satisfies `|z_p − z_q| ≤ B(p,q)`; a pair that does not (KASE's parallel beside a 2 % runway) is reported `fronting_cap_infeasible` and the rows on that path are EXCLUDED from §5's promotion — Q-133 stays the owner's.

**The runway's share enters in two evaluations (numbers: two Dijkstra passes over the pair graph, no solve).** (i) `I⁰_b` with every `β_R = 0` (runway at `z¹ᵃ`). Non-empty → `D_b` chosen in it (below), `Λ_c` and `β_R` follow (§1 (2)-(3)); the runway Bands only ADD slack, so `D_b` stays feasible and the flex is spent by the solver on smoothing the route. (ii) `I⁰_b` EMPTY → `D⁰_b` = the bound nearest the median of the contacts' `z¹ᵃ` (the branch's residual rule, KEEP), `β_R` from `D⁰_b`, and `I_b` recomputed with the widened runway terms: non-empty → `D_b` chosen in `I_b`, the block is HELD (the runway's 20 % ENABLED the pad — 30as's stated purpose); still empty → residual as (i) below. No third pass: `β_R` is then a constant of pass 1b.

**Derivation site.** ONE function `constraints/no_step.hold_interval(planar, law, cs, z1a)` replacing `runway_reach_band_values` (`no_step.py:167-188`): a `RouteGraph` (`routes.py:130`, `budget :175`, `csr :189`) built from the `Diff` rows of `cs`, read by `reach` (`:902`) through the branch's super-source walk (`_reach_super`, KEEP). Called in `solve_design` between pass 1a and pass 1b (`design.py:844-867`); output = ONE hard `Band` per datum column (`model/constraints.py:114`, KEEP) + ONE hard `Band` per runway-family column of every pulled runway (§1 (4)) + the `runway_flex` records (§6) + sidecar `platforms[].reach_band` / `reach_width_m` / `reach_gap_m` (`publication.py:573`). The mint-time DEM test (`pad_blocks.plan_blocks :583`) keeps choosing the CUTS only (30n) and certifies nothing.

**The datum.** `D_b` is CHOSEN at the derivation site — the point of `I_b` nearest the median of the block's contact values `z¹ᵃ_c` — because `β_R` (a Band, a constant of pass 1b) needs `Λ_c` before the solve; the datum column is then a `Pin` of pass 1b. "The datum comes from the feasibility graph" (30y (2)) with no DEM proxy deciding it (30u (b) closed); the trends at 30 / 3 shape the apron around it, not it. (Amended from "the solver chooses": the only freedom that changes hands is the trends' pull on one column per block.)

**EMPTY interval.** (i) The block is a RESIDUAL: `D_b` = the bound nearest the median of the contact bands' mid-points (branch mechanism, KEEP); a contact whose band excludes `D_b` keeps its hold PRICED at 300 under the now-hard fronting caps (§5) — the apron comes as close as the caps allow, the rest is carried by the collar and REPORTED (`pad_frontage_infeasible`, `families.toml:226`, with `reach_gap_m`); never a silent collar (30y (4)), never a runway move. (ii) T2 pre-registered: `building280` ONE block (30z (4)); `I⁰` expected EMPTY (5.99 m over 344 m = 1.74 % > 1.5 %); whether the runway's 20 % of `Λ` (evaluation (ii)) closes the 0.83 m shortfall is the lane's number to post, not to decide; the lane posts the gap, held/residual (branch 45/18), site feet vs 20/107 (branch) and 31/108 (control). The further cut along wall lines (30n, narrowest neck) is the NEXT round's, after the owner reads those numbers. (iii) Any other unit with an empty interval and ≥ 2 blocks: STOP.
