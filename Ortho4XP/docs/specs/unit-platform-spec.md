## §0 WHAT LANE `platform` MEASURED (main 5b2f1d5b, key `[building_pad] unit_platform` OFF) — the premise

- A pad FACE IS RIM-ONLY: 0 interior planar vertices (SPJC `building5`, HECA `building13`); the sim's terrain inside it is the triangulation of its rim. So a pad's surface is wholly decided by its rim.
- Under 23a the rim is mostly the APRON: SPJC `building5` 450 of 535 rim vertices (84 %) are apron-shared; the current SPJC frame (`nestedpads/SPJC_lane.graded.json`, main be2dfd43) reads `building8` 73,439 m², **513 of 549 (93 %)** shared. HECA `building10` (T3 district, 151,828 m²) 253 of 928 (27 %); `building13` (T2, 74,310 m²) 54 of 494 (11 %).
- Because airside is king (23a weld, 14ay: "touching means the same elevation"), "one platform" and "airside moved 0" cannot both hold ON THE RIM. Pricing the plate over its own vertices only (`unit_platform` ON) makes a plane but leaves the apron rim off it: HECA T3 apron-shared rim p50 1.41 / max 4.92 m off the platform; SPJC own plate ~3 m above the apron-dominated median, `within_shape` 1,791 → 12,147. The apron coming to the plane (jetway strip, §30 (4) reach) measured 48 → 1,793 rows at SPJC (#4). Both roads are closed; the third is a region INSIDE the footprint.
- **THE RELIEF THE COLLAR MUST CARRY (measured on the registered graded frames, scratch `collar2.py`: apron-shared rim z minus the pad's OWN-rim median):** HECA `building10` p50 +1.69, **max +4.68 / min −1.74 m** (71 shared vertices > 3 m); HECA `building13` p50 −1.53, min −2.74; SPJC `building8` p50 −1.23, **max +2.45 / min −5.19 m** (16 > 3 m). Against a §20 least-squares plane the maxima fall (the own-median is a worse fit than the plane the spec uses); the bound stands at ≈ 5 m.

## §1 THE PLATFORM REGION (28a (1)) — three rings, one new shape class

1. **THE PLATFORM LEVEL is one plane, tilt ≤ `pad_slope_max` (1 %).** It is §20's least-squares fit of the unit's AIRSIDE frontage (the senior apron leads, `pad_frontage_level`; juniors reported) — the level that MINIMISES the rim relief — and NOT the pad's whole-rim median (17t) nor the pack's absolute zero. The pack's floor zeros (`base_y`, footed walled bodies, `obj8_split_report`) decide only HOW MANY platforms a unit gets: ONE where they agree within `floor_split_m` (0.5 m; HECA T3 813 footed bodies 98 % within −0.66…+0.36, T2 all within −0.70…+0.30, SPJC 84 of 89 within −0.15…+0.47 — one zero each); where the pack authors distinct zeros the unit is split into as many platforms at the pack's offsets (§16g (10) (1), read from the pack, never the DEM). A pad that fronts no airside keeps §20's DEM datum as its platform level.
2. **THE INNER RING** = the unit footprint (the 23a pad polygon, `cluster_pad_faces` / `_pad_polys`) ERODED by the collar width C. C is the §31 (7) bank: `C = clamp(max_rim_relief / bank_slope, bank_min_width_m, platform_collar_max_m)` with `bank_slope` 1:3 (`emit.design.bank_slope`, never a second copy), `bank_min_width_m` 5 m, and a NEW `[building_pad] platform_collar_max_m` = **15 m**: the measured bound ≈ 5 m × 3. `max_rim_relief` is the largest |rim z − platform plane| over the pad's WELDED (airside-shared) rim, read from the stage-1 solve (the welded rim is stage-1's, unchanged by construction). C = 0 where the relief is under `hard_tol_m` (the platform is the plate; today's behaviour). Where erosion by C leaves no inner ring (a pad narrower than 2C, or one whose area falls under `cluster_pad_min_m2`), NO platform is minted, the pad stays today's welded plate and the census prints `platform_refused` with the relief (REPORT, never a gate). A platform never extends outside its pad.
3. **THE COLLAR** = the annulus between the pad rim and the inner ring. It is a `building` face with a HOLE (the emitter's `faces[].holes` already exists) graded as a TERRACE from the welded rim (outer, airside-led, moved 0) to the inner ring (on the platform plane): a `Linear` row per collar cell at `bank_slope` (the §31 (7) bank — a slope, never a cliff), priced as the groundside terrace law prices a bank (`groundside_ramp_max`'s pattern), with the rim vertices as one-way LEADERS and the inner ring's vertices hard on the platform plane. The collar is UNDER the building (inside the footprint): §31 (3) landside-visual, invisible from the cockpit, and it never carries a rolled-on role, so it mints no MOTION row. Its outer edge is the 23a weld (step 0) — the collar is why the rim can stay welded while the interior is one plane.
4. **INTERIOR VERTICES ARE EMITTED — the first pad with interior geometry.** The inner ring is a new ring of the planar map (`planar/shapes` + `weld_cells`, spacing `min_distinct_spacing_m` like any rim); the platform face inside it stays rim-only (a plane needs no interior points). MESH COST: one vertex per rim spacing around the inner ring, so ≈ the pad's own rim count again: HECA `building10` +≈ 900 vertices, SPJC `building8` +≈ 550, T2 +≈ 500; whole-airport ≈ +2 k on 35 k (≈ +6 %), inside the 10-min tile budget (§31 (5)); the bar is the plan/constraints stage ≤ +5 % (§6).
5. **THE OBJECT STAGE seats the unit on the PLATFORM plane** (sidecar `cluster_pads` gains `platform_level` + the inner ring; `plan_unit_datums` reads it instead of the pad ring's median). Contents (§48 / `contents.py`) and carried bodies ride it (§16a). A wall standing in the collar or on the rim is seated on the platform too (one rigid unit, §16f (7)); its foot off the local ground (≤ the relief, ≤ 5 m, at the apron frontage line) is the §16f (2) "held up by the family" residual — REPORTED per body as today, never re-seated. This residual is the price of airside-unmoved, and it is the owner's call (§5 Q1).

## §2 THE SOLID-CONNECTOR DISCRIMINATOR (28a (2)) — one derivation, two readers

A §16g (6) connector (span ≥ `connector_span_m` 200 m, end-ground step ≥ `visual_m`, ends in two units — `footprint_connector._is_connector`) is **SOLID** — and JOINS its two units into one unit with one platform — when ALL THREE hold. **A plan-WIDTH test W is REFUTED by the HECA population and is NOT a test**: the six expelled connectors' oriented hulls (scratch on `obj8_split_report --json`, `ridercensus` frame; 32-point hull samples) are 2-D blobs, not bars — `metal_strip_1_yellow` b50 281 × **142** m, the rail `concrete_3` b1 1,130 × **76** m (curved: its hull width is not its deck width), `concrete_3` b0 478 × 84, `titles_1` b0 294 × 29, `T3_61` b0 178 × 113, `T3_60` b0 161 × 127, `Titles_not_metal` b6 172 × 134. No W separates the strip (142) from the rail (76) in the right direction.

|---|---|---|
| S1 walled along its WHOLE length | every plan cell along the body's long axis holds a part with `Part.height_m` ≥ `chain_min_height_m` (2.5 m) — the per-cell solid height 28a (11) accepted, read on the body's own cells; a run of ≥ 2 cells with only a plate (deck on piers) breaks it | `metal_strip_1_yellow` b50 (11 feet, 281 m): walled; `concrete_3` b1 (84 feet, 1,130 m): a deck on piers — plate cells between the piers; the lane MEASURES this per body and posts the six verdicts |

Today `plan_clusters` cuts nothing — that is the §16g (9) disagreement that lifted T2 +21 m. `pad_cluster_mismatch` (CRITICAL) is the twin that proves the two readers agree.
