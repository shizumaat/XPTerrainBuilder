THE DESIGN (15 lines)
1. Inputs are the base's solved constants only: STATIONS on every standing
   ring within the follow reach, sampled by `planar/chords.stations`, classed
   road / apron / pad / lot / band / structure by the existing role readers.
2. CONSISTENT = `|dz| <= cap * (d - knives)` pairwise (McShane): a
   pairwise-consistent station set always admits a cap surface, so follow
   rows and cap rows cannot conflict inside such a part.
3. 04u STEP CUT: greedy level groups (sorted `(z,x,y)`), nearest-station
   Voronoi partition per group, mint floors (merged slivers = reported
   conflicts), a KNIFE strip of ground `terrace.separation_m + 0.5 *
   identity.min_distinct_spacing_m` = 0.75 m between parts of two groups
   (two shapes, distinct nodes, declared gap joint — three law inequalities
   in one twinned expression).
4. 06d LOT CUT inside a part with road + apron stations > 1.0 m apart: field
   `L` (nearest road level) and `A, d_A` (nearest apron); ramp band where
   `L - A > cap * d_A`; the contour is a shared BREAKLINE (one shape, no
   step); lot `gap:<k>/lot`, ramp `gap:<k>/ramp<j>`, step parts `gap:<k>/s<j>`.
5. Lot rows (the only new law): hard `|z - L| <= transverse(0.02) * d_road`
   over the whole lot + soft `lot_fit` target `L` through `preferred_z`;
   generated by `gap_follow` (one more branch), groundside tier.
6. Everything else unchanged: late cells, followers, `late_fixed`,
   `solve_late_stage`, follow rows; existing movers 0 by construction.
